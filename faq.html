Escape Road - Frequently Asked Questions

🎮 I just opened the page — how do I actually start?

The car is already rolling the moment the page finishes loading. Tap A / D or ← / → the instant you see the road and you're in it — no start button to hunt for.

🕹️ Is there more to the controls than left and right?

That's genuinely it on keyboard — ← / → or A / D. On a phone or tablet, tapping the left or right half of the screen does the same job.

🚧 What actually ends a run?

Clipping a traffic car, hitting a roadblock, or getting boxed in by a police car 🚓. Any one of those stops you cold.

🏆 What am I actually working toward?

Distance and coins. The longer you stay clear of the police, the higher your score climbs 💯, and coins picked up along the way go straight toward unlocking your next car.

📱 Does it feel different on a phone versus a laptop?

The controls swap to touch, but the chase itself — the police, the traffic, the roadblocks — plays out the same either way.

⚡ My run is stuttering — what's going on?

Usually a browser tab overloaded with other background tabs. Close a few, reload the page, and it's normally back to full speed. Chrome or Edge tend to run it smoothest.

👤 Do I have to sign up for anything?

No sign-up, no login screen 🎮. You land on the page and you're driving — accounts only ever matter if you want a score saved between visits.

📊 What's actually driving my score up?

Every second you stay ahead of the police adds to it, and every coin you grab adds more on top. A clean run beats a fast-but-reckless one.

🔋 Is this going to eat through my phone battery?

It's a light browser game, so it's gentler than most native apps, but any moving screen sips power. Dimming your screen a notch stretches things further 🔋.

📩 Something's broken or you want to reach the team?

Head to the Contact Page and describe what happened. Someone reads every message that comes through.
